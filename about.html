Katerina Magkaniari Mitropoulou (Magarini) is an artist, designer and developer. With a foundation in architecture, she plays with physical structures and digital spaces. She holds a MFA in Digital Arts from the Athens School of Fine Arts (2023).

In her work, she attempts to connect individuals through shared experiences and collective meaning, exploring embodied sensations and the poetics of the everyday. Characterized by collaborative and playful elements, her work has been showcased in exhibitions and publications, including the Athens Digital Arts Festival, Primarolia Festival, metamatic:taf, School of Commons, and installations in public spaces in collaboration with Goethe Institute and OTE Group Telecommunications Museum.
